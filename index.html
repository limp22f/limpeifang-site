About

Lim Pei Fang

The hardest deal I ever closed was the one with myself.

She spent close to twenty years in mergers and acquisitions, deal structuring and investing. For a long time it was the only identity she had.

When a 2021 restructuring put her role at risk in the same year Covid-19 hit her family hard, that identity began to slip away, and for the first time she felt completely helpless. She calls what followed the hardest deal she ever had to close, the one with herself.

Out of that turning point she founded Inner Channel and developed I CLEAR, the six-step method in The Invisible Loop. She does not coach from outside the pressure her clients carry. She lived inside it for close to twenty years. Corporate life does not reward good intentions. It rewards results you can point to, and that is the standard she holds her work to today.

She shares her story openly so you can see that yours can change too.

Pei Fang is a coach, mentor and licensed NLP Practitioner based in Singapore, with close to twenty years in mergers and acquisitions, deal structuring and investing. She also practises energy work through Pranic Healing, and Chinese Metaphysics.
